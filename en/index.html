How it works

Six phases, one point of contact from start to finish

Every deal follows the same path, designed to keep the time and the decisions left for you as small as possible.

01

Private sourcing

We find high-potential properties through a network of selected agencies, who flag us the best opportunities before they even reach the open market. It doesn't have to be a villa: an ordinary flat can be worth three times as much once renovated, if the location is right.

02

Team and locked-in quote, before you buy

Before you even buy, we find the right professional for the job and put together the site team, who provide renovation quotes covering every cost. So you know exactly what the whole deal will cost before you commit your capital.

03

Full ownership, in your name

Once the property is chosen and the works budget is set, you complete the deed directly in your name: the property is always and only yours, we never buy or resell it ourselves. From here on, we handle all the operational work that usually holds back would-be property investors.

04

Finishes and furniture bought directly, at the best price

We personally select windows, flooring, tiles, appliances, kitchens and furnishings, with the same technology and the same brands we'd want in our own home: quality names, with real technical substance. We do chase the best price available at the time though, buying directly from the supplier or online, on the hunt for the best deals.

05

The site, followed step by step

We coordinate the professionals, the contractors and the suppliers, with regular site visits and constant reporting: you stay informed at every moment, without needing to be on site.

06

Sale and final report

Through the same network of agencies, we put the property back on the market and agree the asking price together, to get the best possible margin: you're always the seller, just as you were the buyer. We close the deal with a clear report of costs, timeline and result.

Returns

The margin is only part of the result

Every deal lasts 6 to 8 months, and its outcome depends above all on the project. The return for the investor, though, can change a great deal depending on how the deal is structured.

The project

The margin comes from the gap between what a property is today and what it can become: a flat with a garden in town, an old farmhouse in the countryside, a villa with decades-old finishes. You buy at the right price, renovate from a clear quote, and resell. What matters is how much value you can create relative to what you paid.

Bank leverage

With a mortgage on the purchase, the same deal needs less capital of your own, and the return calculated on that capital can look very different from an all-cash purchase. It's a possibility real estate offers quite naturally, to be assessed case by case with your advisor: interest is a cost, and leverage also amplifies less favourable outcomes.

Tax incentives

Buying as a private individual, for example as a second home, can unlock relief on the purchase and on renovation works that has a real impact on the net result. It depends on your personal situation and on the rules in force at the time: we check it with you and your accountant before the purchase.
